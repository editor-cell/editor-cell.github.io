Which languages keep the record

A public figure can be careful and still stop at the border of the language it was released in.

Global · 8 February 2026

Type size

An official document released by the state is only as public as the borders of its language. From school guidance to census notes, any record published in a language not spoken in a city's homes is formally public but practically closed. UNESCO highlights this gap by noting that a large share of learners are not taught in a language they understand well.1source 1 Statistical databases suffer from the same bottleneck; for instance, while the UN Sustainable Development Goals database tries to keep indicators in more than one UN language, the depth of translation remains uneven.2source 2 The true width of a public claim is measured by the number of languages in which it can be opened and read.
